Home is the modules

The spec inverted on 1 August, so the screens invert with it. Five recording surfaces and the last handful of entries are the app; the coach is zero to three rows above them, and most opens it has nothing. The row shape from 3a survives intact — hairline, steel dash, answers on the row, answered rows leave and nothing is promoted. Everything that assumed a plan, a queue or a session runner is cut.
