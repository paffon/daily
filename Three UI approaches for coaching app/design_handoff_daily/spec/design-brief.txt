DAILY — TURN 4 BRIEF

The spec was reversed on 1 August. Recording is now the product; the coach is a
layer above it that is absent on most opens. Turn 3 was built for the opposite
arrangement, so most of it needs re-cutting rather than tweaking.

The four docs in uploads/Daily/docs were all rewritten on 1 August. Re-upload
them before working from this brief — the copies in the project are the old
version and contradict everything below. Once they are current, DESIGN.md §3 and
the Reversals table in RULES.md list what flipped, and this brief is what those
reversals mean for the screens.


THE ONE CHANGE EVERYTHING ELSE FOLLOWS FROM

Home is no longer the coach. Home is the way into five recording surfaces —
workout, nutrition, movement, dance, body — plus the last handful of entries,
so it is visible at a glance that the thing is recording.

The coach stack sits above them: zero to three rows, in the row shape 3a already
uses. Usually zero. When it is zero, home is the modules and the recent entries
and nothing else — no label announcing the silence, no earned sentence required
to fill the gap.

So 3a inverts. Today the questions and "up next" are the screen and "log
something else" is a footer link. Now the modules are the screen and the coach
is the thing that is sometimes there. Keep the row shape exactly: hairline,
steel dash, answers on the row, answered rows leave and nothing is promoted.

3a needs a fourth state, and it is the most important one to draw: nothing to
say. That is what most opens look like now.


DELETE

- "up next", everywhere it appears (3a, 3d, 2a, 2b, and the strip in 2c). It was
  fed by the week plan, and there is no week plan any more. Nothing replaces it.
  Objectives are targets, not a schedule, and must never be drawn as three dated
  rows.

- The backlog count and its second hairline — "gym · 2 more" in 3b. The coach
  caps at three and the floor is set high, so there is no queue to work off. A
  counter implies a backlog that should be cleared, which is the opposite of how
  this is meant to feel. 3b becomes just: answering removes the row, the list
  gets shorter, nothing slides in.

- Rung 3 in 3d — the line lifted into the full-size slot with the list still
  under it. Escalation is now the specificity cascade plus one direct line, with
  nothing stored in between. Keep rungs 1 and 2 (same position, sharper wording)
  and keep rung 4 exactly as drawn. Renumber to two states: "sharper wording"
  and "the direct line".

- Two of the four tuning taps in 2a and 3d. "less often" and "good question" are
  gone. Only "not now" and "never" remain.

- The whole meal container in 3c and 2d: the "← dinner" strip, "3 items so far",
  "in this meal", "add · done", "done · 3 items". See NUTRITION below.

- "battery · not run yet" from the progress list in 3c and 2e.

- 2c, the session runner, as a separate mode. See WORKOUT below.


NEW FRAMES NEEDED

Workout is the biggest gap. The only workout screen that exists is 2c, and it
assumes weight × reps everywhere. Running has no weight box and the design has
nowhere to put a distance.

  Each exercise declares a kind, and the kind decides which fields a set row
  shows:
    loaded      weight × reps            42.5 × 10
    bodyweight  reps, optional +/- load  12   ·  12 +10  ·  8 -20
    hold        duration, optional load  45 s ·  60 s +10
    distance    distance + duration, optional incline   5 km / 28 min
    machine     duration + level, optional distance     20 min @ 8

  Draw at least loaded and distance side by side, on the same screen shape, so
  it is obvious the fields change and the screen does not.

  The number of sets is not a field — it is how many rows have been filled.
  "+ set" copies the row above it, so three sets at one weight cost three taps.
  Draw the copy behaviour; it is the whole ergonomic argument.

  Every row carries a next-time mark — more / same / less — and this replaces
  the "breeze / okay / hard" control drawn in 2c. It is an instruction to a
  future reader rather than a rating of how the set felt, because a rating has
  to be interpreted before it is useful and interpreting it months later at the
  machine is work at the worst possible moment. A set reads "47.5 more".

  The existing +/plain/- notation stays as the fast input for it: + is more,
  blank is same, - is less. "30--" stays legal and means what it looks like.
  The marks are what Previous hands back, so draw them in the Previous strip
  as prominently as the weights.

  Keep the comment field and keep it carrying forward.

  There is no separate runner mode now. The same screen logs live and logs
  afterwards, because the timestamp is editable and Previous is always there.
  So 2c's one-set-at-a-time flow is replaced by the set list itself.

Body. A small module, currently nowhere — weight exists only as a coach question
and photos only as a line in the progress list. Two entry types: a weight and a
timestamp, and a photo. That is the entire module.

Objectives. A new surface, currently nowhere. A short editable list of targets —
"3 workouts a week", "2 dance sessions a week", "protein up", "weight stable" —
plus a free-text statement of intent above them that the app never parses. It is
the coach's only reference point beyond gap arithmetic. It is not a plan and a
missed target is not a debt, so nothing here gets a progress bar, a ring or a
red state.

Editing a past entry. "Edit past workout" was asked for by name, but it applies
to every module — including the timestamp, at any time after the fact. Draw it
once, generically, from the recent-entries list on home.


CHANGE IN PLACE

NUTRITION (3c, 2d). No meals. Nothing in the app knows what dinner is. The
module is a flat timeline of what was eaten and when — six entries across a day
and one entry across a day are the same shape, with no container, no session,
and no "done".

  - An entry is a timestamp, a food, an amount, and a level. The timestamp shows
    and is editable, because logging the apple from an hour ago is the normal
    case.
  - The amount carries the food's own unit, which belongs to the food and is not
    chosen per entry: pizza in slices, coffee in cups, chicken in grams. Show
    the unit next to the number.
  - "light" is renamed "lean". The three levels are lean / normal / loaded.
  - Each food has a default level, so the common entry is one tap and lean or
    loaded is the exception reached for.
  - Levels need examples attached, and this is the part with no drawing yet:
    picking a level should be able to show what it means for this food. Pizza —
    lean: thin crust, light cheese, vegetable toppings. normal: plain cheese and
    tomato, standard slice. loaded: thick crust, meat, extra cheese. Examples
    are optional per food and most will be blank, so the level control has to
    look right with and without them.
  - Drinks are foods. Coffee, beer, juice belong in the same list.
  - Numbers recorded are calories and protein, both optional, both on the normal
    case only. Nothing else.

DANCE (3f). Intensity labels change from easy / okay / hard to marking / social
/ full-out — the real dance words rather than generic ones. Drop "in the plan"
from the strip; there is no plan. Everything else about 3f is right.

MOVEMENT (3e). Right as drawn. Keep stroll / steady / brisk. Add a visible,
editable timestamp per segment, same as nutrition — logging yesterday's walk
today is normal.

SITTING (3g). Right as drawn. It now lives inside the movement module rather
than standing alone, because it is the second entry type there: a segment is an
event, a sitting block is a proportion of a span.

PROGRESS (3c, 2e). Right as drawn, minus the battery row. It now lives inside
each module rather than hanging off home.

NAMING. The module is "workout", not "gym". The strip label in 3a and the
module tile both change.


KEEP, AND DO NOT LOSE WHILE RE-CUTTING

- Previous, everywhere. "last time · 1 · normal", "last time · 18 min · 5.0
  km/h", "previous · 12 july" with the form-cue quote, "last four" on dance.
  This is the single most valuable thing in the design and it now applies to
  every module without exception, at every point of logging.

- The row shape, the hairlines, the steel dash, the two-tap common case with
  last time's values already filled in.

- Rung 4 exactly as it is: alone on the screen, the only place the app says
  "you", with a concrete correction and a way to switch the line off.

- The honest empty lists — "4 entries · not enough to draw", "not run yet".
  Libraries now ship seeded with defaults, but history still starts at nothing
  and the app should keep saying so plainly.

- No grams as a universal unit, no totals, no running counts, no rings, no
  budgets, no streaks.


NO UNIVERSAL DIFFICULTY RATING

Dance and movement carry no rating of how hard they were. Nothing is being
progressively loaded on a commute, and the app is explicitly not trying to
improve the user's dancing. Movement records a speed and dance records an
intensity — both are levels, both say what the thing was, and neither needs a
second axis on top. So 3e and 3f each end up with one scale, not two.
